# Transformers: Snacktime in Disguise

### Assumptions

- We have a clean kitchen counter with the following: 
 - two slices of white bread laid flat and side by side on top of a clean plate
 - a jar of JIF peanut butter
 - a jar of Welch's Grape Jelly 
 - a clean spoon on the counter
 - a napkin
- The robot has an unobstructed access to all previously listed items.
- The robot is charged, fully functional, and ready to folow instructions.
- The robot has 2 hands
___
### Step by Step
1. remove (screw of) the lid from the peanut butter and place the lid on the counter
2. remove (screw of) the lid from the jelly and place it on the counter
3. pick up the spoon
4. scoop up some peanut butter from the jar onto the spoon
5. drop peanut butter from the spoon onto the left slice of bread
6. evenly spread the peanut butter onto the left slice of bread using the rounded side of the spoon
7. pick up the napkin and wipe excess peanut butter from the spoon
8. set down napkin
9. scoop up some jelly from the jar onto the spoon
10. drop jelly from the spoon onto the right slice of bread
11. evenly spread the jelly onto the right slice of bread using the rounded side of the spoon
12. pick up the napkin and wipe excess jelly from the spoon
13. set down napkin
14. set down spoon
15. replace the peanut butter on the peanut butter jar and the jelly lid on the jelly jar
16. Place left bread onto right bread with the peanut butter and jelly sides touching
17. Throw away the used napkin
18. return peanut butter and jelly jars to their starting positions.
19. Task completed as the sandwich is constructed and the workspace is clean
